Ropedoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nar Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Ropedoku (the "Game"), a free-to-play puzzle game supported by interstitial and rewarded video ads. By installing or playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

The Game and the third-party services it uses may collect aggregated, non-personal information, such as:

  • Gameplay data: levels completed or failed, time spent, session length and frequency.
  • Device data: device model, operating system version, language settings.
  • Advertising data: IP address and advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), collected by ad partners.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Game, balance difficulty and fix bugs.
  • To serve and measure advertisements through third-party ad networks.

3. Third-party services

The Game integrates third-party SDKs which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google Play Services
  • Unity Ads / Unity Analytics
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation and the ad networks it connects, which may include AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, Vungle (Liftoff), InMobi, Start.io, Pubmatic and similar partners.

4. Advertising and your choices

Ads shown in the Game may be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can limit this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising settings.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you may change your choice later where the Game offers that option.

5. In-app purchases

Any purchases (such as removing ads) are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment or billing information.

6. Data retention and security

Aggregated data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or to opt out of its sale or sharing. Because the data we hold is anonymized and cannot be linked to you, we generally cannot identify records to act on such requests. Uninstalling the Game stops further data collection by the Game. For data held by ad partners, please use the opt-out controls above or contact those partners directly.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions, such as the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
